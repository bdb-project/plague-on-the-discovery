Plague on the Discovery
By Gianluca Girelli
For Z-code interpreters

Solution by Garry Francis
----------------------------------------
This is a minimal solution. You'll need to EXAMINE lots of things for this solution to make sense. Some optional (but significant) commands are shown in parentheses. These can be omitted once you know what to do.

When the game starts, you are being pursued by security droids and there is a distinct smell of gas in the air. Your first task is to escape from the droids. You can find a flamethrower nearby, but any attempt to use it will cause the gas to ignite, so your second task is to get rid of the gas.

When you vent the gas, you will die in the airless atmosphere if not wearing a helmet, so you need to find a helmet before venting the gas. This is just a taste of what's to come. Timing is crucial, so you may need to learn by dying a few times. The puzzles all have logical solutions, but they're very cruel and you can expect to die many times while trying to work out those solutions.

Your ultimate goal is to work out what happened aboard the Discovery, find the data related to this and a vaccine. Finally, escape from the Discovery and return these to Earth without getting your own spaceship blown up during the process.
